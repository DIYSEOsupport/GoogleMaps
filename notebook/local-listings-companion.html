What is Domain Authority (DA)?
A standard score (1–100) predicting how much search engines trust a site — near 100 is massive trust (Google, Facebook), near 10 is a small, unknown site. Google cross-checks your Name, Address, and Phone (NAP) across the web — listing on high-DA sites creates a trust rub-off effect for your profile. Citation Integrity Rule: match your NAP letter-for-letter across every submission — even "Street" vs. "St." can dilute your ranking signals.
