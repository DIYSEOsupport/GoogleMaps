Google's 3 Ranking Pillars (In Plain English)
  • Relevance: How closely your business details match what a customer typed into Google search.
  • Distance: How close your verified location is to where the searching customer is standing.
  • Prominence: How active, well-known, and trusted your business is (measured by reviews, photos, updates, and listings across the web).
