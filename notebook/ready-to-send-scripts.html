01

The 3-Part Automated Review Outreach Funnel

Turn happy customers into Google reviews without pestering anyone.

Touchpoint 1: Peak Delight
Timing: Within 1 hour of transaction completion · Channel: SMS or direct invoice link
Hi [Customer Name]! Thanks so much for choosing [Business Name] today. If you have 30 seconds, would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? It makes a massive difference for a local family business like ours: [Insert Your Direct Google Review Link] — Thank you!
Touchpoint 2: The Gentle Nudge
Timing: 48 hours later · Channel: Email follow-up
Subject: Just checking in, [Customer Name]! Hi [Customer Name], Thank you for trusting our team with your [specific service]! Our goal is to deliver a perfect 5-star experience every time. If we met that standard for you, could you take just 30 seconds to share your experience on Google? Direct Link: [Insert Your Direct Google Review Link] Your feedback helps other local families find us. We truly appreciate your support! Warmly, — [Your Name], [Business Name]
Touchpoint 3: The Value Check-In
Timing: 7 days later · Channel: Final email
Subject: Quick question about your [Service/Product]... Hi [Customer Name], I hope you're having a fantastic week! I wanted to check back in and make sure you're still completely happy with our work on your [specific service]. If anything needs adjusting, just reply to this email and we'll make it right immediately. If you're 100% satisfied, would you mind writing a brief review for us on Google? It tells Google we're still actively serving our local community: Direct Link: [Insert Your Direct Google Review Link] Thank you so much for your time and business! Best regards, — [Your Name], [Business Name]

03

The Local Partner Cross-Promotion Outreach

Replaces the original agency resale sequence — there's no reseller here, just a way to grow your own visibility for free.

Build a small referral network with nearby, non-competing businesses that share your customer base (a hair salon and a nail studio, a plumber and an electrician, a bakery and a coffee shop). Same goal as a paid outreach funnel — more visibility, more reviews, more inbound customers — sourced from your own local network instead.
Touchpoint 1: The Introduction
Timing: Any time · Channel: In-person, email, or DM
Hi [Owner Name], I run [Business Name] just down the street/around the corner — we get a lot of the same customers as [Their Business Name]. Would love to connect and see if there's a way we can support each other's businesses. Free for a quick coffee or call sometime this week?
Touchpoint 2: The Cross-Promotion Ask
Timing: Once the relationship is warm · Channel: Email or text
Hey [Owner Name], had a thought — what if we recommended each other to our customers when it's a natural fit? I could mention you when a customer asks about [their service], and you could do the same for us. No cost either way, just helping our customers find good local businesses. I'm also happy to leave you a Google review and tag your business in a post if that's useful — no pressure either way.
Touchpoint 3: Keeping It Alive
Timing: Every 1–2 months · Channel: A quick check-in message or visit
Hey [Owner Name]! Just wanted to check in and say we've been recommending you whenever it comes up — hope business is good on your end too. Let me know if there's ever anything I can do to help.

This costs nothing and doesn't require selling a service you don't offer — it produces the same outcome (more visibility, more reviews, more inbound customers) sourced from your own local network.
