## Section 1: The 3-Part Automated Review Outreach Funnel

Use this 3-touchpoint sequence to turn happy customers into Google reviews without pestering anyone.

### Touchpoint 1: Peak Delight (Invoice Delivery)
**Timing:** Within 1 hour of transaction completion.
**Channel:** SMS text message or direct invoice link (highest open/response rates).
**Objective:** Catch the customer at their moment of highest satisfaction.

> Hi [Customer Name]! Thanks so much for choosing [Business Name] today. If you have 30 seconds, would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? It makes a massive difference for a local family business like ours: [Insert Your Direct Google Review Link] — Thank you!

### Touchpoint 2: The Gentle Nudge (48 Hours Later)
**Timing:** Exactly 48 hours after the service was delivered.
**Channel:** Email follow-up.
**Objective:** Catch customers who saw the first message but got distracted.

> **Subject:** Just checking in, [Customer Name]!
>
> Hi [Customer Name],
>
> Thank you for trusting our team with your [specific service]! Our goal is to deliver a perfect 5-star experience every time. If we met that standard for you, could you take just 30 seconds to share your experience on Google?
>
> Direct Link: [Insert Your Direct Google Review Link]
>
> Your feedback helps other local families find us. We truly appreciate your support!
>
> Warmly,
> — [Your Name], [Business Name]

### Touchpoint 3: The Value Check-In (7 Days Later)
**Timing:** 7 days after completion.
**Channel:** Final email.
**Objective:** Frame the ask as customer care, not a review request.

> **Subject:** Quick question about your [Service/Product]...
>
> Hi [Customer Name],
>
> I hope you're having a fantastic week! I wanted to check back in and make sure you're still completely happy with our work on your [specific service]. If anything needs adjusting, just reply to this email and we'll make it right immediately.
>
> If you're 100% satisfied, would you mind writing a brief review for us on Google? It tells Google we're still actively serving our local community:
>
> Direct Link: [Insert Your Direct Google Review Link]
>
> Thank you so much for your time and business!
>
> Best regards,
> — [Your Name], [Business Name]

## Section 3: The Local Partner Cross-Promotion Outreach *(replaces the original agency sales sequence)*

The original Section 3 was a 3-email sequence for pitching *other* business owners on a paid Google Maps optimization retainer — not relevant here, since this kit is for your own listing, not a service you resell.

What actually helps a single local business grow its own visibility for free is **cross-promotion with nearby, non-competing businesses** — the kind of warm local relationship the business plan's own outreach channels already lean on (Chamber of Commerce, local Facebook groups, Nextdoor). Use this 3-touchpoint sequence to build a small referral network with businesses that share your customer base but aren't your competitors (e.g., a hair salon and a nail studio, a plumber and an electrician, a bakery and a coffee shop).

### Touchpoint 1: The Introduction
**Timing:** Any time — no urgency needed.
**Channel:** In-person, or a short email/DM.
**Objective:** Open the relationship, no ask yet.

> Hi [Owner Name], I run [Business Name] just down the street/around the corner — we get a lot of the same customers as [Their Business Name]. Would love to connect and see if there's a way we can support each other's businesses. Free for a quick coffee or call sometime this week?

### Touchpoint 2: The Cross-Promotion Ask
**Timing:** After the relationship is warm (a call, a coffee, a few messages).
**Channel:** Email or text.
**Objective:** Propose a simple, mutual visibility swap.

> Hey [Owner Name], had a thought — what if we recommended each other to our customers when it's a natural fit? I could mention you when a customer asks about [their service], and you could do the same for us. No cost either way, just helping our customers find good local businesses. I'm also happy to leave you a Google review and tag your business in a post if that's useful — no pressure either way.

### Touchpoint 3: Keeping It Alive
**Timing:** Every 1–2 months.
**Channel:** A quick check-in message or an in-person visit.
**Objective:** Keep the referral relationship active without it feeling transactional.

> Hey [Owner Name]! Just wanted to check in and say we've been recommending you whenever it comes up — hope business is good on your end too. Let me know if there's ever anything I can do to help.

**Why this works instead of a sales pitch:** it costs nothing, doesn't require selling a service you don't offer, and produces the same thing the original Section 3 was chasing (more visibility, more reviews, more inbound customers) — just sourced from your own local network instead of a cold-outreach funnel aimed at other business owners.
