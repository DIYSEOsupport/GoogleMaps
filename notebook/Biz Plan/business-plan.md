# The 30-Minute Google Maps Fix — Business Plan

## 1. Product Positioning

**Buyer:** Solo/family-run local business, 1–15 employees, no marketing staff, no agency budget. Trades, food, personal-care, retail — plumber, lawn care, hair salon, boutique, diner, auto shop. Revenue roughly $150K–$1.5M/yr. Budget for a fix: $0–$50 one-time, not a monthly retainer.

**Core promise:** In one afternoon, without hiring anyone, fix the concrete things Google actually weighs (profile completeness, categories, photos, reviews) and get a repeatable weekly/monthly habit so the fix sticks — with your own before/after numbers to prove it worked.

**3 price points:**
- **$9** — impulse buy. "The checklist," sold standalone on Gumroad/Etsy.
- **$27** — standalone "kit" price (current form). Anchor price, matches comparable Etsy digital templates ($15–$35).
- **$37** — Done-For-You bundle (premium copywriting swipe file + 50-citation directory list). Upsell after the $27 purchase. No live calls — self-contained files only.

**Bundle idea:** add a "50 business types" category cheat sheet pack as a bonus PDF to raise perceived value at $27+.

## 2. Sales Page / Listing Copy

See `sales-page.html` for the full draft (headline, subheadline, problem, solution, what's inside, social proof, FAQ, CTA, 3 alternative headlines).

**Objection handling:**
- *"I can just Google this myself for free."* — True, and it'll take 6 hours across 14 outdated blog posts. This is that research done, in order, with a tracker.
- *"I don't have time."* — Section 1 alone is 15 minutes, highest-impact item in the kit.
- *"How do I know it works?"* — Section 5 generates your own before/after proof.

## 3. Customer Instructions

See `onboarding-email.txt`.

**Format to deliver:** Single `.html` file, direct download link (Gumroad/LemonSqueezy handle this natively) — not email attachment. Self-contained, opens in any browser, ~50KB.

**Before shipping:** strip the device-sync/pairing panel (no backend to support `/api/sync/`) or replace with a note "sync coming in a future update."

## 4. Where To Sell It

| Platform | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Gumroad | Zero setup, instant checkout, built-in discounts | 10% + payment fee |
| Etsy Digital | Huge search traffic for small-business templates | $0.20 listing + 6.5% fee, audience skews DIY/craft |
| LemonSqueezy | Auto tax/VAT handling | Smaller organic discovery |
| Shopify page | Full control, no marketplace cut | You supply all traffic, monthly fee |

**Start with Gumroad** — fastest to launch, no monthly fee. Add Etsy as a second listing once copy is dialed in.

## 5. How To Advertise It

- **r/smallbusiness** — self-promo only in designated/weekly threads, check current rules.
- **Facebook local-business groups** — search "[Your City] Small Business Owners"; most allow one self-promo post/week.
- **Local Chamber of Commerce newsletter** — ask directly, warm distribution.
- **Nextdoor Business posts** — Business/Classifieds tab.
- **Cold email to local businesses** — personalize with their actual listing gaps.

See `promo-posts.txt` for 3 ready-to-post variants (Facebook, Reddit, cold email).

**Paid ads:** thin margin at $27 for cold traffic. Test Meta ads (cheaper/more precise targeting than Google Search at this budget) at $10/day for 5–7 days ($50–70 total) before scaling. Prove organic conversion first.

## 6. Launch Sequence (First 2 Weeks)

- **Days 1–2:** Fix sync panel issue, finalize copy, set up Gumroad at $27, test full buyer flow yourself.
- **Days 3–4:** Post in 1–2 Facebook local-business groups, email Chamber contact.
- **Days 5–6:** Send 10–15 personalized cold emails, track replies.
- **Day 7:** Review week-1 numbers, adjust headline/price if conversion is near zero.
- **Days 8–9:** List on Etsy Digital, adapted copy.
- **Days 10–11:** Post in allowed Reddit thread, Nextdoor Business.
- **Days 12–13:** If organic conversion proves out, start $10/day Meta ad test.
- **Day 14:** Full review — sales by channel, best-performing copy, decide on $37 tier next.
